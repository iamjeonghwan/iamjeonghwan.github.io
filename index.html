01About

I'm a graduate of the M.S. Applied Data Science program at Syracuse University, with a B.S. in Electronic Engineering from Hanyang University in Seoul.

02Selected work
01SECOM
Semiconductor Wafer Defect Prediction
Random Forest · SHAP · imbalanced classification

An end-to-end pipeline (EF-PIPELINE v3.1) predicting wafer defects from sensor telemetry on the SECOM dataset. The core challenge is severe class imbalance — defects are rare — so the work centers on recall and honest evaluation, with SHAP used to surface which sensor signals drive each prediction.

03NLP
Support Ticket Classification & Data-Quality Diagnosis
Text classification · Zipf's Law · label-noise diagnostics

A ticket-classification task that turned into a data-quality investigation. When macro F1 stalled around 0.20, I used Zipf's Law diagnostics to show the synthetic corpus carried significant label noise — reframing a "failed model" as evidence that the data, not the method, was the constraint.
