01SECOM
Semiconductor Wafer Defect Prediction
3-Stage Classification · Balanced RF · SHAP

A 3-stage classification pipeline predicting rare wafer defects from 590 sensor signals (1,567 samples, 6.64% defect rate). The multi-stage approach separates confident decisions from uncertain ones: confident Pass units are released, confirmed Fail units are flagged, and uncertain cases go to human re-inspection. Zero defects escaped the Pass gate.

03NLP
Support Ticket Classification & Data-Quality Diagnosis
NLTK · Naive Bayes · Logistic Regression · Zipf's Law

A 5-class text classification task on 8,469 customer support tickets using NLTK preprocessing (tokenization, stop-word removal, Bag-of-Words). When Macro F1 plateaued at ~0.20 across all configurations, Zipf's Law analysis revealed the dataset was synthetic with weak text-label correlation — reframing a "failed model" as evidence that the data, not the method, was the constraint. A low score, correctly diagnosed, is a finding — not a failure.
